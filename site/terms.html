The online demo · last updated 16 November 2026

These terms cover the demo on this website. Tintype's code and model weights have their own licences, in the repository.

A free, non-commercial demo

The demo is free and offered so you can try Tintype. Use it only for personal, educational, research or other non-commercial purposes, in line with the non-commercial licence of the model weights. Do not build it into a product or service, and do not use it to process photos in bulk: install Tintype instead.

What you may upload

  • Only photos you have the right to share, and only for a lawful purpose.
  • Nothing unlawful, including sexual images of children, intimate images shared without consent, or material that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Think twice before uploading photos of other people. The demo keeps nothing, but you are still sending their image to a server.

Limits

  • Photos only: one JPEG, PNG or WebP file per request, up to 10 MB.
  • From one network: 5 photos per 10 minutes, starting with the first, and 20 photos per day.
  • In total: 2,000 photos per day across all visitors. Daily counts start again at midnight UTC.

Photos the demo refuses before scoring, because they are too large or not a supported photo, do not count. Every photo it accepts counts, including one it cannot read. The demo tells you how many you have left after each photo. Do not try to get around the limits, for example by switching addresses or automating requests. We may change the limits, pause the demo or end it at any time.

What a result means

A result is one statistical signal from a model, and it can be wrong either way. At the demo's setting, Tintype flagged 1.2% of real photos and missed 10.3% of AI-generated ones in its blind benchmark, and it does worse on resized, re-saved or screenshotted copies. A result is not proof that a photo is or is not AI-generated. Do not use it as the only basis for any decision about a person, such as hiring, discipline, grading, moderation, publication or a legal claim.
